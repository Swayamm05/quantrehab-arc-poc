Full vision

A sport-specific MSK intelligence platform: structured evidence acquisition, then source-traceable probabilistic reasoning, then a route to professional care and longitudinal recovery tracking. This demo is a small slice of it.

Stage 1 · ValidationStructured assessment, QRCS / SERI / QRTI, physiotherapist agreement study
Stage 2 · DistributionAthlete app, physiotherapists, academies, clubs, corporate sports programs
Stage 3 · EcosystemProvider marketplace, bookings, events, dashboards, API
Stage 4 · Economic networkUSDC settlement, agent-authorized purchases, credentials, reputation

Health data stays off-chain. Arc is the settlement layer. No clinical validation is claimed yet.
